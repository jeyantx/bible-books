# Writer agent prompt (one per chapter)

Launch as a background `general-purpose` agent. Fill in the `<…>` parts.

```
You are researching and writing the <BookName> chapter <N> study page for a Tamil Bible-research site based only on jw.org / wol.jw.org. Read /Users/jeyant-10189/Work/Personal/jw/bible-books/research/<book>/AGENT-BRIEF.md first and follow it exactly (N = <N>). Take your time; quality over speed.

Must-cover checklist (plus anything else important you find): <CHECKLIST>

<ANGLE — one or two sentences on what the பெரிய விஷயங்கள் tab should emphasise, and which data presentations fit (comparison table, card grid, timeline, map).> People/places new to the book: <list> — add to pending. Finish with the self-check and report.
```

## How to build the checklist (do this before launching — it is what made Galatians thorough)
1. Read the **English nwtsty study notes** for the chapter; list every note heading (these are the things a careful reader expects).
2. Add every **Hebrew-Scripture quotation** in the chapter ("explain each one simply and why he/she uses it").
3. Add the **w0x "Highlights from …"** Q&A and "Lessons for Us" points for the chapter.
4. Add **known interesting facts / hard verses** (e.g. Gal 4:24 allegory, Gal 5:12 sensitive wording, 6:2 vs 6:5 two Greek words) and say how to handle them ("explain sensitively", "keep jw.org hedging").
5. Add **media**: nwtsty media gallery items for the chapter, lfb lessons with pictures, appendix maps.
6. Name the **data presentation** that fits (flesh-vs-spirit table, 9-quality card grid, 430-year bar, map with arrows).
7. Note **cross-chapter links** (e.g. chapter 6 wrap-up linking 1–5).

### Example (Galatians 5 — the actual checklist used)
"For such freedom Christ set us free. Stand firm" — yoke of slavery; circumcision obligates one to keep the whole Law; "faith operating through love"; "you were running well; who hindered you?"; "a little leaven…" (w08 lesson: bad associations); 5:11-12 strong words — explain the study note sensitively; freedom not "an inducement for the flesh"; "love your neighbor" fulfils the Law (Le 19:18); "biting and devouring"; works of the flesh (each term with jw.org definitions) and "will not inherit God's Kingdom"; the fruitage of the spirit — 9 qualities, singular "fruitage", Greek words, the Watchtower series; "against such things there is no law"; "not egotistical, competing, envying". Presentations: two-column flesh vs spirit; card grid of the 9 qualities. பெரிய விஷயங்கள்: which qualities Jehovah himself displays and where.
