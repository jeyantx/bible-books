## Tamil outline (nwtsty)
- இனி அடிமைகள் அல்ல, மகன்கள் (1-7) · கலாத்தியர்கள்மீது பவுலுக்கு அக்கறை (8-20) · ஆகாரும் சாராளும்: இரண்டு ஒப்பந்தங்கள் (21-31) · மேலான எருசலேம் சுதந்திரமாக இருக்கிறாள், அவள்தான் நமக்குத் தாய் (26)
- English book outline: "No longer slaves but sons of God (3:26–4:7)"; "An appeal not to be enslaved to weak and beggarly things again (4:8-11)"; "Paul's concern for Christians in Galatia (4:12-20)"; "A symbolic drama to explain Christian freedom (4:21–5:1)".

## English study note highlights (nwtsty 48/4)
- 4:2 supervisors/stewards: guardianship of minor well known; supervisor legally appointed guardian; stewards manage whole household; heir no more rights than slave; compares to Jews under Law until God's Son released them.
- 4:3 elementary things: “rudimentary elements”, letters of alphabet; (1) philosophies, (2) unscriptural Jewish asceticism/“worship of angels”, (3) Law-keeping for salvation; Christians like grown sons.
- 4:4 full limit of the time: lit. “fullness of time”; Jehovah set fixed time (Ge 3:15; 49:10; 1Pe 1:10-12; Da 9:25); born 2 BCE of Jewish virgin Mary. Under law: kept Mosaic Law; abolished after death (Ro 10:4).
- 4:5 release by purchase exagorazō (also 3:13); adoption — God chooses; Greco-Roman adoption mainly for benefit of adopter, Jehovah's for benefit of adopted.
- 4:6 spirit of his Son = holy spirit sent by means of the Son; Abba lit. “the father/O Father”, endearing; slave could not use it of master unless adopted.
- 4:9 to know God = relationship; “be known by God” = approved; beggarly = lit. “poor, needy”, figuratively “miserable, worthless”.
- 4:10 Sabbaths, sabbath years, new moons, Atonement Day, Passover, Unleavened Bread, Weeks, Booths; Gentile Christians returning to pagan observances would also show lack of faith in ransom.
- 4:13 physical illness: might have been eye affliction (4:15; 6:11; cp. Ac 23:1-5); led to first opportunity; ~47-48 CE; revisited ~49 CE.
- 4:15 gouged out eyes: common figure of speech; especially appropriate if chronic eye affliction.
- 4:19 little children: teknion diminutive = affection (“dear children”); Paul as mother.
- 4:22 free woman: Sarah + Jerusalem above; Israel not free under Law.
- 4:24 symbolic drama = allegory; based on Ge 16-21; covenants “apparently the Mosaic Law covenant and the Abrahamic covenant”; Hagar and Sarah themselves are not the covenants.
- 4:29 persecuting: Ge 21:9 Ishmael “mocking Isaac”; Isaac born through spirit — Jehovah revived reproductive powers; natural Jews persecuted anointed.

## Notes / cautions
- w26.06 adjustment must be followed: covenants = Law covenant & Abrahamic covenant. Older Tamil articles (w06 3/15, w92 6/15) said “new covenant” — not used for that point.
- ip-2 / re / w15 / media caption agree: Sarah (free woman) corresponds to “Jerusalem above” = Jehovah's heavenly, wifelike organization of spirit creatures.
- Eye illness: always hedged (“might”, “may”, “apparently”).
- app.js has no abbreviation for Daniel/Nehemiah/Philemon — written in full inside `.no-refs`.
