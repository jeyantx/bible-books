## Tamil outline (nwtsty)
- எருசலேமிலுள்ள அப்போஸ்தலர்களை பவுல் சந்திக்கிறார் (1-10) · பேதுருவை (கேபாவை) பவுல் திருத்துகிறார் (11-14) · விசுவாசத்தால் மட்டுமே நீதிமான்களாக ஏற்றுக்கொள்ளப்படுகிறோம் (15-21)
- English book outline: B. Paul defends his apostleship (1:10–2:14); C. Declared righteous only through faith (2:15–4:20).

## English study-note highlights (nwtsty 48/2)
- 2:1 “after 14 years” — may mean “in the 14th year”; likely 36 C.E. (first visit) → 49 C.E. (with Titus & Barnabas, circumcision issue, Ac 15:2). => jw.org view: Ga 2:1-10 = the Ac 15 visit (also it-2 “Paul”: “accompanied by Barnabas, Titus… Ac 15:1-29; Ga 2:1-10”; Ac 15 study note on James: “Apparently referring to that occasion… Ga 2:1-9”). The famine-relief trip (Ac 11:29, 30; 12:25, c. 46 C.E.) is a separate trip (Ac 11 study note: famine ~46 C.E.).
- 2:2 “as a result of a revelation” — detail not in Acts; Christ as head apparently used a revelation to direct Paul to take the issue to Jerusalem (~49 C.E.); proves he is a true apostle (Ga 1:1, 15, 16). “preaching” = proclaim as public messenger.
- 2:3 Titus — “a Greek” (Hellen), uncircumcised Gentile; “compel” may suggest Judaizers pressured him; Ac 15:23-29 ruling; Titus served mainly among Gentiles, unlike Timothy (Ac 16:3).
- 2:4 pseudadelphos “false brother” only here and 2Co 11:26 — “a Christian in name only”; “brought in quietly”, “slipped in to spy” = subtle tactics (2Co 11:13-15).
- 2:5 “truth of the good news” = whole body of Christian teaching. Footnote: lit. “an hour”.
- 2:6 Greek mss read “God”; some Hebrew translations use the divine name.
- 2:7 Peter's / Paul's assignments not exclusive: Peter opened to Gentiles (Ac 10:44-48); Paul preached to many Jews (Ac 9:15). Peter later went E to Babylon (1Pe 5:13); Paul W, perhaps to Spain.
- 2:8 energeō “empowered” — authority + capability.
- 2:9 “pillars” — support, solid, reliable (1Ti 3:15; Re 10:1-3). “right hand of fellowship” — partnership (2Ki 10:15); ~49 C.E.
- 2:10 “keep the poor in mind” — the collection: 1Co 16:1-3 (Galatia given directions), 2Co 8, 9 (c. 55), Ro 15:25, 26 (c. 56), Ac 24:17 “gifts of mercy”; Joh 13:35.
- 2:11 “resisted” lit. “stood against”; footnote “he stood condemned”.
- 2:12 meals = fellowship with prayers; Jos 23:6, 7; Joh 18:28; Peter used 3rd key with Cornelius 36 C.E., ate there (Ac 10:48; 11:1-17); ~13 years later stopped; “from James” = associated with James; could undermine Ac 15 decision same year (~49); Paul retells not to embarrass Peter but to adjust the Galatians' view. “those of the circumcised class” (Ac 11:2; Col 4:11; Tit 1:10).
- 2:13 synypokrinomai / hypokrisis — stage actors with masks; “hypocrisy”.
- 2:16 dikaioō, dikaiōma, dikaiōsis — cleared of charge, guiltless, pronounced righteous and treated so (Ro 3:24); Jesus' sacrifice is the basis (Ro 3:19-24; 10:3, 4; Ga 3:10-12, 24).
- 2:18 “things I tore down” — going back under Law rebuilds them → transgressor (Ga 3:19).
- 2:19 “through law I died toward law” — Law condemned him; Law covenant terminated by Jesus' death (Col 2:13, 14; Ro 7:4); Law led to Christ (Ga 3:24).
- 2:20 synstauroō “nailed to the stake along with” (Mt 27:44); “who loved me and handed himself over for me” — “me” = personal benefit (Joh 3:16); motivated Paul to be loving (2Co 5:14); Mt 9:12, 13.
- 2:21 “died for nothing”; trying to earn life = rejecting undeserved kindness (Ro 11:5, 6; Ga 5:4). Footnote: “shove aside”.

## Reviewer notes
- 2 Peter date: si "Bible's inspired penmen" chart (https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1101990061) — Peter, First and Second Peter c. 64 C.E. → Antioch incident (~49) to 2Pe ≈ 15 years. Kept.
- nwtstg "Judaizers" (https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1001077413): the word "Judaizers" is not in the Greek Scriptures; found in Josephus; Paul used a related verb (ioudaizō) at Ga 2:14. Page reworded accordingly.
