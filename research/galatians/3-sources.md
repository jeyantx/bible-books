## Tamil outline (nwtsty)
- திருச்சட்டத்தின் செயல்களும் விசுவாசமும் (1-14) — விசுவாசத்தால் நீதிமான் வாழ்வு பெறுவான் (11)
- ஆபிரகாமுக்குத் திருச்சட்டத்தின் மூலம் வாக்குறுதி கொடுக்கப்படவில்லை (15-18) — கிறிஸ்துதான் ஆபிரகாமின் சந்ததி (16)
- திருச்சட்டத்தின் ஆரம்பமும் நோக்கமும் (19-25)
- விசுவாசத்தால் கடவுளுடைய மகன்கள் (26-29) — கிறிஸ்துவுக்குச் சொந்தமானவர்கள் ஆபிரகாமின் சந்ததி (29)
- English outline: Works of law versus faith (3:1-14); promises to Abraham not through Law (3:15-18); Purpose of the Mosaic Law (3:19-25); No longer slaves but sons of God (3:26–4:7). Section C of book: declared righteous only through faith (2:15–4:20).

## English study-note highlights
- 3:1 senseless = anoētos — "unwillingness to use one's mental faculties"; not lack of intelligence. Galatians = southern Galatia congregations. "brought you under this evil influence" = baskainō, only here; sometimes "bewitch/put a spell on", but figurative: leading astray, wrong influence of misleaders.
- 3:3 "after starting on a spiritual course" lit. "in spirit"; "finishing on a fleshly course" lit. "completed in flesh" — influenced by men not guided by spirit; could endanger eternal life (Ga 6:8).
- 3:5 ftn "performs miracles". 3:2 ftn "learn from".
- 3:6 Jehovah — quote of Ge 15:6 has YHWH; Greek mss "God" (perhaps from LXX); also Ro 4:3; Jas 2:23. Tamil ftn → App. A5.
- 3:7 sons of Abraham — covenant of circumcision made with Abraham (Ge 17:10); false brothers argued one is a son of Abraham only by keeping the Law; Paul: those with faith like Abraham.
- 3:8 Ge 12:3; 18:18.
- 3:9 pistos — "who had faith" / "faithful".
- 3:10 De 27:26; Jews agreed to the Law (Ex 24:3); epikataratos = condemned by God; Jews needed redemption from Adam's sin AND curse of Law.
- 3:11 Hab 2:4 — faith in Christ, not works of Law, basis of being declared righteous (Ro 10:3, 4; Ro 1:17 note: Hebrew ʼemunah "faithfulness", related to ʼaman; Paul may quote LXX pistis; Ro 1:17; Heb 10:38).
- 3:12 Le 18:5; De 30:16; Ro 10:5.
- 3:13 De 21:22,23 — bodies of "accursed of God" hung on stakes; Jesus hung on stake as cursed criminal; took full weight of curse; Jews who put faith in him relieved; may echo Joh 3:14 (bronze serpent). Marginal 1Co 7:23; Mt 26:27,28; 1Pe 2:24; Ac 5:30.
- 3:14 Eph 2:15,16; Joe 2:28.
- 3:15 diathēkē 33 times, always covenant/agreement; some render "will" but context → covenant.
- 3:16 Christ primary offspring; sperma; Ge 3:15; Abrahamic covenant (Ge 12:1-3,7; 13:14,15; 17:7; 22:15-18; 24:7); David line; 3:26-29 secondary part. "seeds... seed" plural vs singular; not Ishmael or Keturah's sons; Isaac → Jacob → Judah → David; 1st-c. Jews expected one person (Lu 3:15; Joh 1:25; 7:41,42); also thought themselves God's children (Joh 8:39-41).
- 3:17 430 years — Abrahamic covenant took effect 1943 B.C.E. when Abraham crossed the Euphrates on way to Canaan (Ge 12:4,5,7), apparently 14th Nisan; Ex 12:41 "430 years… on this very day" → 1513 B.C.E. Law covenant did not invalidate the Abrahamic covenant; added; directed people to Christ. Hebrew berith >250 times.
- 3:19 "was added" = temporary nature; "to make transgressions manifest" — exposes Israel and all as sinners (Ro 5:20; 7:7-11); sacrifices reminders (Heb 10:1-4, 11); "until" — Law not forever (Ro 7:6). "transmitted through angels" — Hebrew Scriptures don't say; Ac 7:53; Heb 2:2,3; Jehovah the Lawgiver. Mediator = Moses; mesitēs 6 times (Ga 3:19,20; 1Ti 2:5; Heb 8:6; 9:15; 12:24); inaugurated covenant (Ex 24:3-8), installed priests, >600 laws, pleaded for people (Nu 16:20-22; 21:7; De 9:18-20, 25-29).
- 3:20 no mediator when one person: Abrahamic covenant unilateral promise, no conditions; Law covenant two parties. "God is only one" echoes De 6:4.
- 3:22 custody of sin — "enclose together, hem in", like net of fish (Lu 5:6).
- 3:23 "handed over into custody" — guarded by Law to the faith.
- 3:24 paidagōgos "child leader", "tutor"; also 1Co 4:15; guardian not the teacher; protect, family standards, discipline; Law showed sinfulness → need for Messiah (Ac 4:12). Tamil ftn: வே.வா., “ஆசானாக.”
- 3:25 faith arrived; Jesus only one who fulfilled Law perfectly; "Perfecter of our faith" (Heb 12:2).
- 3:27 baptized into Christ — anointed ones, one body (1Co 12:13; Ro 6:3); "put on Christ" — so possessed of the mind of Christ (Col 3:10,12; Ro 13:14). Tamil ftn: “கிறிஸ்துவின் குணங்களைப் பின்பற்றுகிறீர்கள்.”
- 3:28 Jew/Greek — no distinction by race; "Israel of God" (Ga 6:16; Eph 2:11-18); Galatia mix of Jews, Greeks, Romans, locals. slave/freeman — all bought with blood (1Co 7:22,23; 1Pe 1:18,19).
- 3:29 secondary part of the offspring = 144,000 (Re 5:9,10; 14:1,4), mostly from Gentiles (Ac 3:25,26).
