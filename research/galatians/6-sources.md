## Study notes (English, summarised)
- 6:1 false step = paraptōma, "a fall beside"; from mistake in judgment to serious sin; heading wrong way. spiritual qualifications = pneumatikos — must show they are guided by holy spirit (5:16,18,25). readjust = katartizō — restore to proper condition; Mt 4:21 "mending" nets; related noun katartismos (Eph 4:12) used in medical texts for setting a bone. "try to" — result depends on the one counseled. keep an eye on yourself — switch plural→singular; warning against giving in to the same temptation and against self-righteousness (1Co 10:12).
- 6:2 burdens = baros (plural), "heavy things", "troublesome things"; consequences of false step. law of the Christ = all Jesus taught + what spirit directed followers to write; replaced Mosaic Law (Jer 31:31-34; Heb 8:6-13); from Jehovah (Joh 14:10); only here; cf 1Co 9:21; Jas 1:25; 2:12; Ro 3:27.
- 6:5 load = phortion, something carried without reference to weight; personal load of responsibility to God; "military term for a man's pack or a soldier's kit".
- 6:6 katēcheō "to sound down" (oral instruction); share material and spiritual things; student who applies & becomes teacher shares "good things" (2Ti 2:2).
- 6:7 mocked = gesture with the nose, turning one's nose up; danger of thinking God's principles can be evaded. sow/reap: well-known proverb from agriculture; usually negative, Paul also positive (Pr 11:18; 22:8; Ho 8:7; 10:12; 2Co 9:6).
- 6:8 flesh → works of the flesh (5:19-21) → corruption (Ro 5:12; 8:21). spirit → living so God's spirit operates freely → everlasting life.
- 6:9 give up = grow weary / lose enthusiasm (2Co 4:1 note).
- 6:10 opportunity = kairos "favorable time" (Eph 5:16). related in faith = household (family) of faith; congregations met in homes (Ro 16:3-5; Eph 2:19).
- 6:11 Paul usually dictated (Ro 16:22); this apparently written himself. footnote: "Or possibly, 'with what a long letter.'"
- 6:12 make a good impression in the flesh = look good outwardly; motive: favorable impression on Jews, avoid persecution; in effect denying that Jesus' death was the only basis for salvation. torture stake = stauros.
- 6:14 world put to death toward me & I toward the world.
- 6:15 new creation = each anointed Christian, spirit-begotten son of God (4:6,7); also the congregation "Israel of God" a new spiritual creation (2Co 5:17 note: no new material things since 6th day, but new spiritual things).
- 6:16 rule of conduct = kanōn, from Hebrew qaneh "reed" measuring (Eze 40:5). Israel of God = spiritual Israel, not natural; Ho 2:23; Ro 9:6, 22-27; Israel = "Contender (Perseverer) With God"; imitate Jacob's faith (Ge 32:22-28; Heb 12:16).
- 6:17 brand marks = stigma (plural), only here; brand marks to identify slaves; possibly scars from persecution (2Co 4:10; 11:23-27; Php 3:10) or his way of life/ministry.
- 6:18 spirit you show = dominant mental inclination; Greek plural "you" — whole congregations.
- Eyesight: nwtsty 2Co 12:7 note: "Some statements ... raise the possibility that Paul suffered from problems with his eyesight" (Ga 4:15; 6:11; Ac 23:1-5) https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/b/r1/lp-e/nwtsty/47/12 ; nwtsty Gal 4 notes: "Paul might have suffered from an affliction of the eyes" https://wol.jw.org/en/wol/d/r1/lp-e/1001070766
