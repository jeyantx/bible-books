## English study note highlights (nwtsty)
- Title “To the Galatians” added later; P46 papyrus.
- 1:2 Galatia: first tour c. 47-48 CE; Timothy from Galatia; 1Co 16:1; 1Pe 1:1.
- 1:4 “system of things” (aiōn = age) — began after Flood; Christians rescued by ransom.
- 1:6 Judaizers, “false brothers” (2:4), feared persecution (6:12,13); claimed Paul not real apostle; Ac 15 decision already given.
- 1:7 “causing trouble” = stir up, unsettle (Ac 15:24; 17:13).
- 1:8 anathema = “laid up / set apart”, like votive offering; LXX for cherem “devoted to destruction”. 2Jo 9-11.
- 1:10 peithō = persuade/win approval; Paul adaptable in method (1Co 9:22) never in message; 1Th 2:4.
- 1:12 apokalypsis = uncovering; learned directly from Jesus like other apostles (1Co 9:1).
- 1:13 “Judaism” only in Ga 1:13,14; hyperbolē = “to the point of excess”.
- 1:14 paradosis = tradition handed down; son of Pharisees, Gamaliel (Ac 22:3).
- 1:16 “flesh and blood” idiom = human.
- 1:17 Arabia not in Acts — complements Luke; maybe Syrian desert; quiet meditation.
- 1:18 “three years” possibly parts of three; c. 36 CE; historeō = visit to get information.
- 1:19 James = Jesus' half brother, apostle in broader sense (sent one of Jerusalem cong.).
- 1:21 Syria≈Antioch area, Cilicia≈Tarsus; c. 36-45 CE ~8 yrs little known; 2Co 11:23-27, 2Co 12:1-4 maybe during this period.
